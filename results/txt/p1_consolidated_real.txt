MECHANISM HETEROGENEITY (Reviewer R1)
================================================================================
Interpretation guide:
  - Entropy family (TENT, SHOT): dominated by majority non-anomalous nodes.
  - Pseudo-label: gradient flows from BOTH confident-anomaly and confident-normal
    pseudo-labels, so it is NOT majority-dominated in the same way.
  - BN-adapt: zero-gradient statistic adaptation.
If Pseudo-label / BN-adapt behave DIFFERENTLY from TENT/SHOT, the degradation is
NOT a generic property of 'all adaptation' but specific to normal-dominated
entropy objectives — which is the narrowed, honest claim of the revision.
